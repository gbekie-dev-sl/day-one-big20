Everything changed in 2022. From that point on, we became inseparable. Looking back now, I'm endlessly grateful for how things turned out, because having you as my best friend has been one of the greatest parts of my life these past four years.

As you step into your 20s, I wish you real growth, peace of mind, unshakeable happiness, and massive success in everything you set your heart on. May this decade open every door you've been working toward and bring you everything you deserve.

Thank you for being my best friend through all of it. Here's to 20 years of your life — and to many more standing side by side as real ones.
